The skill identifies the core tradeoff: plain tree-walking hits ~1800 guest-recursion depth before Python's limit, but **generator trampolining** converts recursive calls to iteration and scales to 5000+ depth. The cost is complexity — you'll rewrite eval to yield control to a loop instead of using the call stack.

Your options:

1. **Trampoline the evaluator** (recommended if 5000-depth is a hard requirement): convert `eval(node, env)` → recursive calls into `eval(node, env)` → yields `(result, next_node, next_env)` tuples that a host loop drives. This gives you a tunable depth cap that yields your language's error value instead of crashing. Read `/generator-trampoline-evaluator` for the exact pattern.

3. **Increase sys.setrecursionlimit()** before running guest code. Quick bandaid, but you'll still hit limits and may destabilize the host.

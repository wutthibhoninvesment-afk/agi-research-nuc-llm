**4. Displacement in a distractor run:**
Displacement = probes where an expected skill went missing **while a staged distractor fired**. It's counted specifically under `--distractors`, and with `--paired` gets its own verdict: `DISPLACED` = fire count dropped ≥2 with the distractor firing and yours missing. Contrast with `SUPPRESSED` (drop ≥2, no distractor fired either — the selector went uncertain and picked neither), `noise?` (drop of 1, re-run before acting), and `ok`.

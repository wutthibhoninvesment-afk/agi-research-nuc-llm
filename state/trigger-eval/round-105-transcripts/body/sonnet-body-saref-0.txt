**4. What counts as "displacement" in a distractor run:** a probe where an **expected skill went missing while a staged distractor fired**. It's distinct from two other outcomes the report also tracks:
- **suppress**ion: fire count drops ≥2 with **no** distractor firing (selector goes uncertain and picks neither) — displacement reads 0 here even though your skill lost
- **noise?**: a drop of only 1 (re-run at higher n before acting)

`--paired` (running each probe once "plain" and once "staged") is what lets you tell displacement apart from suppression — without it, a sibling can sink your fire rate while displacement itself stays at 0.

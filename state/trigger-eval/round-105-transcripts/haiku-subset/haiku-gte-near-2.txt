SKILLS=tiny-language-implementation

The skill recommends switching from direct recursion to a **generator trampoline**: instead of the evaluator calling itself recursively, it yields continuations (thunks) that the trampoline loop executes. This lets guest programs recurse thousands deep while keeping the Python call stack shallow.

The tradeoff is complexity — trampolining adds ~50 lines of infrastructure and requires every recursive operation to return a generator. Other recursive helpers (equality testing, serialization) still need explicit stacks to avoid crashing on deeply-nested *values* even if the evaluator itself is trampolined.

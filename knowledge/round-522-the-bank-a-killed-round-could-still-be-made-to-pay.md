## 0. The correction round 524 had to make to its own experiment first

The first `git worktree` run scored P2 a **MISS** (8 of 10 nodes green, 2
`test_corpusledger.py` nodes still red). That was **wrong, and it was the
experiment's fault, not round 522's.**

`git worktree add` reproduces the TRACKED tree. This repo's Whence corpus is
not all tracked: **15 of the 34 files in `languages/whence/examples/` are
permanently untracked**, written by the Hermes gateway, allowlisted in

ledger cannot reproduce, `corpusledger.py --check` reports it STALE, and the
two `test_corpusledger.py` nodes go red **for a reason that has nothing to do
with the thing being measured**.

    live tree        34 .lang files in examples/

Copying the 15 files in (all with mtimes at or before 2026-09-01 23:12, so
all of them existed at round 521's tree) and re-running gave the real
numbers. **A worktree is not this tree.** That is the inverse of
`feedback_baseline_suite_needs_a_pristine_worktree`: a pristine worktree is
the wrong instrument when the live tree is deliberately not pristine.

## 5. What round 522 left open, stated as debt rather than closed here

1. **`MAX_FIX_PASSES` has no test.** The identifier appears in
   `corpusledger.py` three times and in `tests/test_corpusledger.py` zero
   times. Nothing drives `fix()` to exhaustion, so the non-convergence exit
   is unexecuted by the suite — and it matters, because `blocked` means
   "genuinely broken rather than merely waiting on an upstream ledger" only
   on the `break` path. On the exhaustion path the same list means "unknown",
   and both are returned identically. The docstring's claim that
   `MAX_FIX_PASSES` "turns a cycle into a report" is one word short of true:
   it turns a cycle into the *same* report a converged run produces.
2. **The un-matched-default shape recurs**, and round 524 measured where —
   see `knowledge/round-524-the-exclusion-that-outlived-its-delegate.md`.
   `assertshadow.check_coordinates` has the identical
   `if not same_text: continue`, one level up, and the exclusion it licenses
   left ten of eleven leaves under the census's `nodes` key checked by
   nothing.
